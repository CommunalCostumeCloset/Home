Before You Borrow

Once pickup is confirmed, the reservation can no longer be cancelled. The costume will become available again only after it is returned and confirmed by an admin.

After physically picking up the costume, click “Confirm Pickup” right away. Otherwise, the system may still show it as being in the closet and another student could reserve it by mistake.
